Real app, software GL, 1100 x 700, seed 7, Medium quality unless noted, one homestead with a finished cabin, 30 game days of the real sim after a real clock crossing of the hit. Harness: `scripts/feltShots.mjs` (see its header); images are in `shots/felt/<kind>/` (gitignored, each with `sheet.png` and `result.json`), world-only reruns in `shots/felt-w/`, Low and High in `shots/felt-low/` and `shots/felt-high/`. Every kind's contact sheet was viewed.

## 1. Defects, ranked

1. **The day card does not fit 1100 x 700 for 16 of 22 kinds** (ash_winter, asteroid, bioweapon, cyberattack, dam_flood, emp, financial, grid_down, invasion, lawless, mega_quake, nuclear, solar_flare, unknown, wildfire, zombies). It scrolls inside a card centered in the viewport, so the masthead and headline are cut off at the top; for financial and zombies the "Face it" button sits under the bottom edge. Fix: cap `maxHeight` at `calc(100vh - 32px)` with the scroll on the card body and the button pinned, or shrink the body text. `src/ui/DayCard.tsx` line 33 (outer `alignItems: center`, inner `maxHeight: 100%`).
2. **Decision cards are generic for most kinds.** "Who holds the road" for lawless, civil_war, invasion and zombies; "The place after the shock" for mega_quake, dam_flood, wildfire and supervolcano; "The numbers on the shelf" for drought, ash_winter, asteroid and crop_blight; "Protect what still works" for solar_flare, grid_down and EMP; "Keep the sickness out" for pandemic, bioweapon **and livestock_plague** (a barn disease offering masks and a clinic visit: wrong). Only cyberattack, financial, nuclear and unknown have their own. `src/sim/felt/cardLibrary.ts` (`genericCards`); a kind's `spec.cards` wins, so each content package needs to add its own.
3. **The radio is nearly silent in week 1.** 21 of 22 kinds heard exactly 1 line in 7 days (EMP heard 0), against a checklist floor of 5 distinct lines; 8 kinds heard no checkable claim at all by day 7 (lawless, civil_war, drought, pandemic, zombies, emp, crop_blight, livestock_plague). The lines heard are often the generic "a group raiding farms is working this side of the county" or the nuclear "Creek, bad. Water, bad downstream" on kinds it does not fit (bioweapon, asteroid, supervolcano). The played checklist item `radio` (5 distinct lines in 30 days) fails only for the EMP, but the first week is the one that matters. `src/sim/felt/feltRadio.ts` (`FELT_SHARE` 0.65, eligibility) and `src/sim/radio.ts` (hear chance); the generic bulletin set in `radioLines.ts`.
4. **The world mostly reads as a colour grade, not as marks.** From the cabin at eye level, no checkpoint, flag, column, wall crowd, pyre, barricade or tape is readable for lawless, civil_war, invasion, zombies, pandemic, bioweapon and livestock_plague; marks sit on the road (about 35 units away) and are small. **Broken cues:** drought still shows a full creek at day 31 (`dry_bed` is decals only, FL2 note), crop_blight shows healthy green fields at day 7 and 31, dam_flood shows no water by day 7 (the plane drains in the first day, 0 ft). Good: quake cracks and tents, ash_winter, supervolcano and asteroid ash, wildfire's orange sky and smoke in week 1 (gone by day 31). `src/sim/felt/marks.ts` (`RECIPES`), `src/game/felt/` (placement, prop scale), `FloodPlane`, stream level.
5. **Low quality hides most of the felt layer.** At Low, mega_quake and dam_flood have no decals (cracks, mud), 14 to 17 props and diff only 3.9 % eye / 2.8 % aerial (under the 4 % floor); wildfire at Low has 0 props, 0 fire sources and a plain bright sky (no grade on Low). Medium and High are identical in marks (48 / 45 / 42) and diff. That matches the doc's Low rule, but the floor says "at least one visible change", and Low fails it for the two natural kinds.
6. **Hit moment is text over a live HUD.** The letterbox runs over the full HUD (side panels, top bar, toolbar stay visible), captions overlap the bottom toolbar, and only the EMP shows a real white flash; solar_flare, nuclear, supervolcano, ash_winter, asteroid have no tint or darkening on the frame. Titles that are too long wrap to two lines and overlap the left panel: unknown, crop_blight, livestock_plague (and bioweapon runs edge to edge). Five kinds use the generic caption "Something is very wrong." (lawless, bioweapon, unknown, crop_blight, livestock_plague). `src/ui/felt/HitMoment.tsx` (hide `.hud`, set `data-testid` panels `visibility`), `src/sim/felt/hitMoment.ts` (`HIT_BY_KIND` texts, tint per beat).
7. **Popups sit over the view.** The goal-completion popup ("GOAL 2 OF 5 DONE: The lights come on. Power for the pump, the lights and the freezer.") appears over the Board and radio panel for cyberattack, solar_flare, wildfire, drought, crop_blight and emp, including kinds where it is not the kind's goal (the first-hour power goal is still ticking). The "First wave survived" milestone popup is not a `role=dialog` and covered the month-1 view for lawless, mega_quake, nuclear and unknown. Neighbor and "distribution point" decision cards also come up on top of the world (the harness now answers them before a world shot). `src/ui/` milestone and goal toasts.
8. **Do-first steps without a Do-it button:** "Seal the place, or stay open?" (pandemic, bioweapon) and "Lights out and quiet, or go to the road?" (zombies) show text only; the other steps work. They come from `KindFx.goal` / `guidance.suggestions` entries with no `act`. `src/sim/felt/hitCard.ts`.
9. **A floating prop in the sky:** ash_winter month-1 eye shot shows a dark slab hanging at about eye height left of centre (a plank or tarp mark with the wrong height), and the cabin is a speck in the distance (the eye pose is the same as the other kinds, so a prop or ash drift is filling the view). `src/game/felt/markPlacement.ts`, `RoadsideProps`.
10. **Red tags and tarps from `fx.physical.damage` never showed in the quake runs.** Damage records were 0 for mega_quake and dam_flood in the live runs (the harness cabin is set finished by stage, not built), 1 record for ash_winter and supervolcano (a tarp). Not a verdict on WP6; it needs a run with a built-through cabin. Also: the civil_war feed says "Nothing new today" on day 7 while the ticker reports war news (strictly-true feed, but flat), and the planned kinds share one hit title "Everything changes today".

## 2. Checked live and fine

- **Hit moment:** 22 of 22 raise the overlay and show a title beat at 1100 x 700 (frames taken with a frozen clock, so they are deterministic); it ends on its own and the day card follows. The earlier "no hit" for civil_war, invasion and cyberattack was the harness reusing `dayCardSeen` from the previous kind, not a game bug.
- **Order-package gate cards** (lawless: tollkeeper, posse, watch committee, salvage; civil_war: checkpoint, requisition, informant, fugitive, partisan; invasion: checkpoint, draft notice, requisition, informant, lost soldier): all draw, all fit 1100 x 700, every answer button changes the state (no dead buttons). `draft_notice` could not be made in the civil_war state (no draftable man at that moment). `GateCard` has no `role=dialog` (harness finds it by text).
- **Tech felt cards** (solar_flare, grid_down, EMP, cyberattack, financial): cards and goal chains are present, options show costs and odds, disabled options say why ("needs a powered ham set"). The Disconnect and Faraday goal steps tick from state.
- **Radio scouting:** every checkable line that was heard (1 per run) scouted to **confirmed**; no false line came up in week 1, so "false" was not seen live (it is unit-tested). Open claims stayed 0.
- **Cyber Verify:** the "Verify on ham" button needs a ham rig, own power and radio skill 2; in the cyber state (grid flickering, shortwave only) it never shows. Gated, not dead; not verified live.
- **Goal card:** present on day 1 for all 22 kinds with a working Do-it on the current step. **Board:** opens, shows "What's different" and what was heard, fits, no raw tokens (`undefined`, `NaN`) in any card.
- **Perf:** felt layer adds 1 to 2 draw calls and 192 to 724 triangles at the day-7 shots (limit 6 and 6k).
- **Text:** no raw `undefined` or `NaN`, no metric units seen in the cards. Console and page errors: none beyond harness noise.

## 3. Harness and checklist notes

- `scripts/feltShots.mjs` flags: `--kinds a,b|all`, `--quality`, `--quick`, `--world`, `--gates`, `--night`, `--audit`, `--summary`, `--min`. A kind takes 2.5 to 4 minutes in software GL; run 3 or 4 kinds per call under `flock /tmp/cb.lock`.
- The contact sheet is built in the page; on a heavy page it came out blank once, so the shots are also kept as separate files. A Pillow stitcher is a 15-line alternative.
- `checklist.ts` `playedChecks` (world, hitBeat, card, goal, radio, teeth) is pinned in `feltChecklist.test.ts`: wildfire and unknown fail `teeth`, EMP fails `radio` (3 lines in 30 days), 7 planned kinds are `-`. `FELT_ENFORCE` is unchanged (empty). The hit-title uniqueness rule fails the 5 planned kinds that share "Everything changes today".
