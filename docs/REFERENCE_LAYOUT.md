# Inspiration image (one possible compound — not a target)

The user shared an aerial concept as **inspiration for the spirit** of the game. It is *one way* a compound could end up, **not** the design target: do not design toward reproducing it. Players should be able to build very different compounds (hidden in the woods, dug into a hillside, a fortified farm, a village of families, a mostly-underground site …). What this one shows, back to gate:

- **Setting:** a clearing in dense conifer forest; a gravel drive comes in through the trees to a single **main gate** (steel bar gates) in the front wall.
- **Perimeter:** a **shipping-container wall** stacked 2–3 high (dark painted) on all four sides; **observation towers** at the corners (steel lattice legs, a cabin, floodlights) and one beside the gate.
- **Family housing (left column):** the main **log house** (two storeys, big gable window wall, wraparound deck), **Mom & Dad's** cabin, **Sister's** and **Brother's** small cabins, the **Grandparents'** single-storey cross-gabled house with porch. Lawns, a few kept trees, split-rail fences.
- **Vehicles:** a **mobile home / RV** on a gravel pad (solar on its roof), a **defense truck** (armored pickup) on a pad by the gate.
- **Utilities (right column, along a central gravel spine):** **garage** (two bays) with generator / tank boxes beside it, **fuel storage** (two horizontal tanks), **medical room** (red cross), **food warehouse** with grain / water silos, **greenhouse** (glazed), **vegetable garden** raised beds, **chicken coop**, **water filtration** shed with **rainwater tanks**, a **solar array** (ground racks) and a **battery storage** shed.
- **Underground shelter entrance:** a camouflage-netted hatch near the front wall.
- **Threat:** figures (raiders / the infected) gathering outside the wall in the woods.

Notes taken from it (as possibilities, not rules):
- One readable pattern: a gravel spine from the gate, housing on one side, services on the other. Other layouts must work as well (see ROADMAP "Layouts").
- Every building has a furnished interior (user request: "make sure buildings have interiors fully furnished too like a real building would"; "for future buildings make sure those are interiored too"). Interiors use the shared kit in `src/game/buildkit/interior.ts`.
