| 2026-10-23 | Next builds (user picked all) | Order: **more buildings** (workshop / garage, root cellar, chicken coop + run, garden beds, log house, **outhouse**) → **perimeter + sightlines** → **water + power** → **underground bunker**. Every building has a furnished interior. |
| 2026-10-23 | Plumbing and electricity | **Allowed on higher-tech, self-sustaining compounds, but only through real infrastructure that costs something.** Running water needs a source (well / spring / cistern), pressure (gravity tank or pump), pipe runs, fixtures and waste handling (outhouse → composting toilet → septic tank + leach field). Electricity needs generation (solar / micro-hydro / wind / generator + fuel), batteries, an inverter and wiring. Each costs materials, labour, know-how (someone with plumbing / electrical skill, or slower and riskier without) and upkeep (pump wear, battery ageing, frozen pipes in winter, fuel). Interiors show the tier: oil lamps, hand pump and outhouse at first; faucets, a water heater, a flush toilet and electric lights once a building is connected. |
| 2026-10-23 | What the perimeter protects against | **Both:** (1) **events that test you**: wildlife (bears, coyotes after the hens), trespassers and thieves, and later raids during a crisis, picking targets by what they can see and reach; fences, hedges, lights and dogs deter or delay them, and you see the outcome; (2) **real-time raids**: attackers visibly approach and you respond (defenders to positions, lights, gates). Build order: perimeter pieces + sightlines → threat events → real-time raids. |
| 2026-10-23 | Sightlines | **Both views:** an exposure heatmap (visible from the county road, from the ridge, at night from your lights) with a per-building "seen from road %", and a click-a-point viewshed for towers, gates and posts. Terrain, trees, hedges, berms and walls block sight. |
| 2026-10-23 | Lived-in spaces | Every building is dressed with seeded clutter (dishes, books, toys, boots, pets, pictures, woodpiles, laundry lines …) so no two homes look alike. |
| 2026-10-23 | Scale | **Everything, now and later, is to human scale** (user). 1 unit ≈ 6 m, a person 0.28 ≈ 1.7 m; furniture and props are authored in metres × M. `src/game/scale.test.ts` guards doors, storeys, furniture, clutter, animals and perimeter heights: add a line there for every new piece, and check new work with a street-level shot that includes a person. |
| 2026-10-23 | Water + power model (provisional) | **Pipes and cables are drawn lines** (trenched by the family, costed per length) that join buildings into a water network and a power network. Sources: hand-dug well + hand pump (no pressure, water is carried), drilled well (contractor drilling; the pump needs power), rain cistern (fills from connected roofs by season), gravity water tower; waste via outhouse → septic tank + leach field. Power: solar array, battery + inverter shed, generator shed (burns fuel). Each building gets **Plumb it / Wire it** jobs (fixtures / wiring kit + labour; faster and safer with a mechanic) that change its interior (faucet instead of the hand pump, water heater, flush toilet with septic, ceiling lights, a fridge). A **daily balance**: solar by season, demand per connected building, battery state of charge, generator fuel, water supply vs. use, rain into cisterns. Upkeep: battery fade, pump failures (repairs), generator fuel. Shortages are logged (morale effects wait for needs). |
| 2026-10-23 | Graphics correctness (user: "realistic graphics and detail and immersion are very important") | Procedural art must never show impossible geometry: no window or door corners through roof lines or corners, nothing floating or sunk, no z-fighting, nothing through walls. Every gabled shell is checked by `validateSpec` when built (openings inside their wall with a margin, under the eave, clear of each other; gable glass under the rakes, auto-fitted or rake-shaped); `buildings.test.ts` fails on any problem. Add a rule there whenever a new failure mode turns up. Every new piece also gets eye-level and aerial screenshots that are checked before merging. |
| 2026-10-24 | Underground (provisional) | **Bunkers are built room by room** (GDS §2.2 underground layer). A bunker starts at an entrance (a steel hatch with a ladder down a 5 m shaft, or a stairwell under a small headhouse); every other room must share at least one 3 m cell of wall with a room already planned, and gets a doorway through it. All rooms of a bunker share one floor level, the entrance's ground less ≈ 4.8 m; each room needs ≈ 0.9 m of earth over its roof slab and can't be dug deeper than ≈ 6 m. Rooms go under the yard, gardens and houses but not under the creek, wells or the septic field. Rooms: tunnel, buried 40 ft container (the cheap one), bunkroom (8), galley, storeroom, plant room (NBC filter, batteries, generator, 2,000 L tank), medical bay, comms & armoury. Digging uses a hired excavator; the net earth dug out goes on a **spoil pile** that an earth berm draws on (60 % less shovel work); ditches add to the pile. While a room is built the terrain over it is an open pit (dug → walls poured → roof slab, waterproofed → backfilled). **Underground view** (U, or the Underground button; it comes on when you pick a bunker room): see-through ground, trees hidden, surface buildings ghosted, bunker rooms cut open. Walking down into a bunker, the escape tunnel to a hidden exit, and bunker water / power / air (the plant room feeding the rest) come next. |
| 2026-10-24 | Cutaway decor | Things hung on walls above the cut (pictures, signs, lamps, curtains) are a `decor` layer that the cutaway hides with the upper walls, so nothing floats. Lit windows glow only from outside (a one-sided glow over the glass); from inside you look out into the night. Lamplight reaches about its own room (no shadowed point lights → no lit pools on the yard through walls); sheds keep a dim work light at night. |
| 2026-10-24 | Escape tunnel (user: "very securely locked from the inside … outsiders should NEVER be able to bust through from the exit side"; "a looooong tunnel"; "half a mile long or longer"; "dimly lit"; "hidden/buried"; "too conspicuous out there in an open field") | A line drawn from a free wall of a bunker room out to an exit (Underground menu → Escape tunnel), ≥ 60 m and with its exit ≥ 50 m from every building; it may **run on under the neighbours' land** (up to 360 m past the line, +40 h of work for that stretch), so routes of half a mile or more are possible. **Inside** (the user's photo): a whitewashed, board-formed concrete arch you walk upright in (2.2 m wide, 2.6 m to the crown), a wet concrete floor with a drain gutter, a cable run on hooks, and one caged warm bulb every 24 m: dim (only the four nearest bulbs cast light). Cut and cover with a hired excavator, poured in 6 m vault sections (trees stay; only the hatch spot is cleared). The floor leaves the bunker level and follows the land at ≤ 8 % so there is always ≥ 0.9 m of earth over the vault. **Egress only, absolutely:** `canEnter(run, 'outside')` is always false: no event, raid, tool or stat may ever open it from outside (threat systems must call `canEnter` and respect it). The bunker end is a blast door shut in its frame with two steel drop-bars on the room side; the exit is a chamber with a ladder up a concrete shaft to a steel lid with no handle, hinge or hasp outside (hinges, drop-bolts and locking wheel on the underside), its collar sunk below grade. **Hidden:** in the trees it is buried under soil, a thick layer of leaves, fallen branches, a fallen trunk and brush; in open ground it sits under a hand of turf, flush with the grass, so nothing shows. Walk mode: along the tunnel, up the ladder and out from below; from above, standing on it does nothing. Rules: not under the creek, wells, the septic field or other rooms; exit shaft ≤ 9 m. |
| 2026-10-24 | Bunker light (user: "dampen the lights … more warm lighting") | Bunker and tunnel lamps are warm (≈ 3000 K) and low; no daylight underground. |
| 2026-10-24 | Bunker hardening (user: "lined with nuclear proof/fallout survival material, steel cage/faraday cage shield emp, etc. … upgrades that obviously cost more money") | Four upgrades, each in three tiers, ordered per room in the Hardening panel or with "Whole bunker"; the family fits them as retrofit jobs (sim/hardening.ts). **Fallout:** Basic: extra earth and a shielded hatch (PF 40) → Shelter: dense concrete and a baffled entry (PF 1,000) → Military: lead and steel lining (PF 10,000+). An unhardened room is PF 20. **Faraday (EMP):** Mesh cage (70 % of electronics inside survive) → Bonded cage, gasketed doors, filtered entries (97 %) → Welded shell (100 %). **NBC air:** Particulate HEPA and hand crank (keeps out fallout dust) → Gas-tight: carbon and blast valves → Overpressure. **Blast:** blast doors on every opening (15 psi) → reinforced roof and I-beams (50 psi) → hardened shell and posts (100 psi). Small rooms take half the materials and time. Each tier is visible inside: copper strap, rubber door gaskets and a filtered entry box, mesh at the ceiling seams, deep concrete reveals, lead sheets with seams, the HEPA blower, red blast valves, a pressure gauge, green blast doors, I-beams and posts.
