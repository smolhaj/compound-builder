| 2026-10-23 | Plumbing and electricity | **Allowed on higher-tech, self-sustaining compounds, but only through real infrastructure that costs something.** Running water needs a source (well / spring / cistern), pressure (gravity tank or pump), pipe runs, fixtures and waste handling (outhouse → composting toilet → septic tank + leach field). Each costs materials, labour, know-how (someone with plumbing / electrical skill, or slower and riskier without) and upkeep (pump wear, battery ageing, frozen pipes in winter, fuel). | 2026-10-23 | Water + power model (provisional) | **Pipes and cables are drawn lines** (trenched by the family, costed per length) that join buildings into a water network and a power network. Sources: hand-dug well + hand pump (no pressure, water is carried), drilled well (contractor drilling; the pump needs power), rain cistern (fills from connected roofs by season), gravity water tower; waste via outhouse → septic tank + leach field. | 2026-10-24 | Underground (provisional) | **Bunkers are built room by room** (GDS §2.2 underground layer). A bunker starts at an entrance (a steel hatch with a ladder down a 5 m shaft, or a stairwell under a small headhouse); every other room must share at least one 3 m cell of wall with a room already planned, and gets a doorway through it. All rooms of a bunker share one floor level, the entrance's ground less ≈ 4.8 m; each room needs ≈ 0.9 m of earth over its roof slab and can't be dug deeper than ≈ 6 m. Rooms go under the yard, gardens and houses but not under the creek, wells or the septic field. Rooms: tunnel, buried 40 ft container (the cheap one), bunkroom (8), galley, storeroom, plant room (NBC filter, batteries, generator, 2,000 L tank), medical bay, comms & armory. Digging uses a hired excavator; the net earth dug out goes on a **spoil pile** that an earth berm draws on (60 % less shovel work); ditches add to the pile. While a room is built the terrain over it is an open pit (dug → walls poured → roof slab, waterproofed → backfilled). **Underground view** (U, or the Underground button; it comes on when you pick a bunker room): see-through ground, trees hidden, surface buildings ghosted, bunker rooms cut open. Walking down into a bunker, the escape tunnel to a hidden exit, and bunker water / power / air (the plant room feeding the rest) come next. |
