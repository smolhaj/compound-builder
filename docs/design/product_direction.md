# Product direction (owner answers, 2026-11-19)

These are the owner's answers to the "tie the game together" questions. They are the contract for what to build next; revisit when playtests say otherwise.

## Decisions

1. **Core arc: a mix, chosen at the start.** New Game offers different framings, each with its own goals and scoring: survival to dynasty, fortress builder sandbox, and authored scenario campaigns. This needs a framing picker at New Game and per-framing goals, endings and score parts. The rich starts and God Mode already behave as unscored sandbox conveniences.
2. **Domes: late game and rich starts only.** Prices stay as audited (a family glass dome about $33M, an arcology over $1B). Normal runs treat them as a goal that is earned; Founder and Magnate starts open them from day one. No mid-game income shortcut is planned.
3. **Next effort, all four:**
   - **Balance and tuning pass:** bot playtests across disasters and builds against the proposed survival targets; fixes the untuned heat and ice age first.
   - **First-time player experience:** a guided first hour, clearer goals, fewer panels, a tutorial scenario that shows the whole loop.
   - **More content:** disasters, regions, events, buildings and people stories.
   - **Polish and performance:** frame rate on weak machines, load times, save robustness, bug sweep, accessibility and settings.
4. **Systems glue: one clear dashboard plus alerts.** The Compound Board becomes the home screen (power, water, food, air, defense, morale, threats, what needs you now), with everything else as a drill-down.
